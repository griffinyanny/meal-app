Artifact 01 · the app icon

One 1024 artboard, sitting in a grid of thirty other rounded squares

The ember, at 54% of the artboard, centred, at rest. A food-derived mark is not just the category cliché — it is a claim this product does not make. A pot or a fork says "recipes live here"; the ember says something is on and attending to you, which is the actual promise.

§02's 40px mark threshold governs a drawn orb in the UI. An icon is one raster downsampled, so the toque stays at every size — dropping it at 60px would mean shipping two icons.

Artifact 02 · the launch screen

Floor, wash, mark. Half a second of it.

The brief said the splash carries light.ambient; §03 says light.hero is the recipe for "only where the orb is". Hero wins, and the brief gets amended. The splash is nothing but the orb, so the rule already covers it.

It is a static PNG per device size. The orb cannot breathe here, so the ember is frozen at the top of its cycle — full opacity, scale 1.045 — and the first breath happens when the real screen paints. That handoff is the whole reason the wash matters.

What stays out

No spinner. Agreed, and the stronger version of your reason: a spinner would be the first thing a new user sees the chef do, and it would be a lie about work.

No wordmark. The home-screen label already said "Meal" two hundred milliseconds ago.

Status bar and home indicator in. They are what make it read as already open instead of as a card.

No ring, no steam. Same call as the icon: both are 64px+ features and both are motion, which a PNG cannot have.

Orb at 88 — the hero rung, unchanged. It looks small in a still frame and correct in the half-second it exists, because the next screen it hands to draws it at exactly the same size.

Artifact 03 · cut

No install prompt

Two users, both told how to add it by hand before they ever open it. An in-app prompt would spend the product's first act teaching a browser gesture the app is not allowed to perform — to an audience that already knows.

Revisit if it goes wider than that circle, or let the native build make it moot. Nothing else here depends on it.

Artifact 04 · offline

Four characters, and nothing else on the screen changes its mind

Offline is announced once, in one place: a clause appended to the count the header already shows. Meta type, caption colour, no fill and no border — so law 05 never applies and there is nothing to tap, dismiss or ignore. No banner, no strip, no per-row badge, no second sentence lower down.

Things offline genuinely breaks go provisional — fill rgba(240,222,190,.09), line .2, label #A29484 — and keep their ordinary label. They do not each explain themselves; the header already did. §01 has no hue for this and does not need one.

The queue is not counted in the UI. A running tally invites worry about a promise the app has already kept, and only the chef loops — so flushing cannot spin, pulse or shimmer either. One 180ms crossfade to a different word, then a 180ms exit. If the flush takes four seconds, the word just sits there.
