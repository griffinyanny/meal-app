B · THE PICKED MEAL

1g · 3f

Provenance is type, not chrome, and the word is PICKED. NEW Eyebrow DINNER · PICKED with the 10px bookmark, meta names the source. No badge, no accent, no second card design. Not YOURS — a possessive breaks the moment a household has two people. Not SAVED — a chef recipe you favourited is also saved; PICKED names the act. Meta carries who: Griffin’s pick · 40 min · scaled to 3.

1f

The chef answers with a night and a reason, and servings get said exactly once, in the chef’s voice, at the moment of placement — never as a stepper, never twice.

1g

A picked meal’s rationale argues placement, not the dish. The chef’s own meals argue for what it is; a picked meal argues for the night it landed on and the leftovers it spends. Same slot, different job.

1j

The boundary is stated, not enforced silently: it’s your recipe, so I won’t rewrite it. The chef scales and places; it does not edit your method.

1i

Picks survive a regenerate by default, and the guarantee is stated before the ask, not after it. Dropping them is a deliberate second act.

1k · 3m

Too many picks for one week: two options, not three. All four this week or Two this week, two stay in your recipes. The queue-and-remind option is cut — it needed a held-recipe concept, a surface and a reminder. V1.5 backlog. 1k’s layout is unchanged.

3f

One presentation that degrades — no inventory mode. NEW As picks accumulate the summary shifts from claiming the dishes to claiming the arrangement, and keeps doing that all the way up. Cuts 1h’s five-of-seven threshold: at six of seven the chef still picks the nights, still shops, still spends the leftovers.

C · MODIFY, FEEDBACK, FAILURE

1n–1p

Three modify rules. The working and landed ring sits on the changed row’s own radius, never the day container’s. The acknowledgement is the rewritten rationale in place. Rows never reflow — errors are the single sanctioned exception.

1o · 3k

The toast occupies the action bar’s exact slot and geometry. NEW Same 96px offset, same 52px height, same 16px radius, same insets — so it reads as the bar becoming the message, not two objects swapping through one space. It never coexists with the bar. Confirm goes inert while working.

1q–1s

A compact row cannot host the chef’s voice. At three-meal density the row carries title and meta only; the sentence goes to the toast in the bar’s slot. This is why the toast exists at all.

1t

Errors live in the row — destructive text under a hairline with the retry beside it. The row grows; nothing above or below moves.

1u · 3n

A slot with no answer yet is provisional, not loading. No hue, no spinner: rgba(240,222,190,.09) fill, .2 line, a muted sentence where the title goes. Its only control is Decide now. “Leave it to me” is cut — it needs the chef to wake up later. V1.5 backlog.

3g · 3h

Generation is the provisional row, repeated. NEW The full rail arrives in the first second with every day and slot present and provisional, then resolves in place. Progress is a count of written meals, never a bar. No separate streaming vocabulary exists.

1v · 1w

Rating is one tap on the thing you just cooked, and it never blocks the screen. Volume is handled by asking about the week, not each meal.

D · DENSITY, TIME, AND THE BOTTOM EDGE

2a · 2b

Days are containers; meals are inset rows. Fifteen meals fit the same scroll as five dinners. Only dinner carries rationale. Tonight is marked by a time in the eyebrow, not by size.

2b · 2c

A Wednesday morning and a Wednesday evening are different screens on identical data. Morning: no floating action at all, because there is nothing to do yet. Evening: hero lighting, the week below the fold, a real primary and two escape hatches.

3i · 3j

Draft and confirmed are different screens too. NEW A draft has a decision in the floating slot and a warning above it. A confirmed week has no floating action, a grocery row where the decision used to be, and the chef speaking in the past tense.

3o · 3p

A week is the days you chose, never a calendar with holes. Unplanned days are not rows; the absence is acknowledged once, at the bottom, with the one control that changes it.

1l

Tapping a day opens the day sheet. Three meals, their leftover dependencies, and the same door at the foot. 1m’s in-rail expansion is held, not killed — it is the one thing on the still-open list that needs usage to settle.

1x · 1z

The grill wall is two frames, together — the constraint read at plan time and the one at cook time. 1y stays rejected: a third state that restates the second.

spec §06

Exactly one floating layer above the tab bar, and the screen’s contextual primary owns it. No persistent search pill, no FAB, and the toast borrows the same slot rather than adding one.

1g · 2a

Titles never open with a verb — method belongs in the meta, and when one method covers four or more meals the week absorbs it and the cards drop it. A container needs contents: empty days are 56px rail rows with no surface, and dashed boxes are retired.
