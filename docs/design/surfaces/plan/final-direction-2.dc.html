WHAT IS LEFT

Three open questions, one hard dependency, four things parked

Still open

Day sheet or in-rail expansion. 1l ships; 1m is held. One usage question settles it — whether tapping a day is editing or reading.

Gold or cream for the landed ring. Drawn gold in 3k. It touches the three-mark budget for 400ms alongside the header orb. My read: it stays gold, because the change was the chef’s work, and a cream ring would read as a control you could press. Yours to call.

Do the picker’s four tiles push a view or filter in place? A pushed view can hold a section header and a count; filtering in place keeps selections visible. Drawn as pushes; not decided.

Now load-bearing, not invented

Staleness detection is a hard dependency now. It was the highest-payoff invented capability when it only fed 1a’s optional block. The picker opens on it, and the picker is the only ingress — so saved, never cooked has to be a real query before this ships, or 3b loses its first screen.

Servings scaling stays required by 1f — a generation task, not arithmetic, and the meta says scaled to 3 in five frames.

Day-level move (1l) and save-a-week (1v) are still drawn ahead of the spine.

V1.5 backlog — parked, not lost

The queue. Two recipes held for next Sunday with a reminder. Needs a held-recipe object, a surface and a notification.

“Leave it to me.” The chef deciding an open slot later, unprompted. Needs the chef to wake up.

Households. PICKED and the Griffin’s pick meta are drawn to survive a second person; nothing else is.

Divergence. A confirmed week where Tuesday didn’t get cooked. 3j is the calm case; Wave 2 owes the other one.
